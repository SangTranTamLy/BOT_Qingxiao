Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Qingxiao TikTok Analytics and the Qingxiao EJ Discord Bot process information when you use the Service.

1. Information processed

Depending on the feature you use, the Service may process:

  • Discord user, server, channel, and message identifiers needed to respond to commands.
  • Files and images you submit for emoji processing.
  • Public TikTok link information, such as video metadata, captions, thumbnails, and public engagement values.
  • After TikTok authorization: the TikTok account identifier, display name, avatar, profile information, approved account statistics, and public video metadata covered by the scopes you grant.
  • Technical logs needed to prevent errors, abuse, and security problems.

2. Information we do not request

The Service does not ask for or intentionally collect your TikTok password, Discord password, browser cookies, or private messages unrelated to a command or link you send to the bot.

3. How information is used

  • To execute Discord commands and return results.
  • To validate, resize, compress, and upload emoji files requested by you.
  • To display TikTok analytics and authorized account information requested by you.
  • To maintain security, troubleshoot failures, and comply with platform requirements.

4. TikTok authorization and tokens

TikTok authentication is completed on TikTok. The Service receives access only to the scopes you approve. Access and refresh tokens, when used, are intended to be handled server-side and must not be shared publicly. You can revoke access from your TikTok account settings.

5. Sharing and third parties

The Service does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Discord, TikTok, hosting providers, and technical services required to operate the requested feature. Each third party handles information under its own privacy policy and terms.

6. Retention and deletion

Temporary uploaded files are intended to be removed after processing. TikTok connection data is intended to be kept only while the connection is needed, subject to the Service’s operational requirements. You may request disconnection or deletion through the Discord server where Qingxiao EJ is installed.

7. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so do not send passwords, cookies, access tokens, or other secrets in Discord messages.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children who are not permitted to use Discord or TikTok under the rules applicable to them.

9. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the Service changes. The latest version will remain available on this page. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the project owner through the Discord server where Qingxiao EJ is installed.
